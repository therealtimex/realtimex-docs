# Marketplace Operations

The `Marketplace` section in RealTimeX is more than discovery and publishing. Current builds also include in-app operational pages for purchases, published listings, and billing.

Open these surfaces from `Settings > Marketplace`.

## The three main operator pages

Current Marketplace operations revolve around:

- `My Purchases`
- `My Published`
- `Usage and Billing`

These are different from the public Marketplace website and different from the one-time sync or publish flows.

## My Purchases

`My Purchases` is your in-app Marketplace library.

The page currently includes:

- a purchased-item count
- latest purchase summary
- paginated results
- item cards with type, price, author, and tags

Each purchased item can expose actions such as:

- `Open in app`
- `View details`

`Open in app` routes you back into the appropriate RealTimeX management surface for that item type.

### Supported item types

Current purchase cards recognize Marketplace items such as:

- agents
- tools
- plugins
- MCP servers
- local MCP servers
- local apps
- agent flows
- agent skills
- slash commands
- system prompts

### Offline behavior

Current builds cache recent purchase views for offline use.

That means:

- you may still see cached purchases while offline
- detail links and fresh Marketplace data still require a live connection

## My Published

`My Published` is the management surface for items you have already listed in the Marketplace.

The page supports:

- search
- type filtering
- status review
- `Update Publish` actions

Current published-item types include:

- local apps
- local MCP servers
- MCP servers
- agent flows
- slash commands
- agents
- system prompts

### Statuses

Current builds surface statuses such as:

- `Draft`
- `Available`
- `Maintenance`
- `Unavailable`

Use this page when you need to update metadata, cover imagery, tags, or listing state after the first publish.

## Usage and Billing

`Usage and Billing` is the account-level Marketplace billing surface inside RealTimeX.

Current builds include:

- a cloud-status summary card
- top-up actions when billing services are reachable
- billing history
- invoice and receipt access

### Billing history

The billing history view is useful for:

- reviewing invoices
- checking payment status
- opening receipt links

Like `My Purchases`, it can fall back to cached data when you are offline, but fresh receipts and payment actions still depend on a live service connection.

## Marketplace operations vs syncing

Use [Syncing Items](/marketplace-syncing) when you want to bring a Marketplace asset into your RealTimeX instance.

Use `Marketplace Operations` when you want to manage what you already bought, published, or paid for.

## Marketplace operations vs sharing

Use [Sharing & Publishing](/marketplace-sharing) for the initial publish flow.

Use `Marketplace Operations` after publication when you want to:

- review your published catalog
- search and filter your published items
- update an existing listing

## When to use these pages

Use the Marketplace operator pages when you need to:

- confirm that a purchase exists in your library
- jump from a purchased item into the right in-app management page
- review what you have already published
- update Marketplace listings without republishing from scratch
- inspect invoices or top up Marketplace credit

# Meet RealTimeX

RealTimeX is an AI workspace and operations platform that brings chat, retrieval, agent runtimes, local apps, channels, and model management into one product.

You can run it with hosted providers, self-hosted services, or on-device local models. You can also decide how much control you want over approvals, working directories, credentials, automations, and deployment shape.

RealTimeX started from strong open-source roots and has expanded into a broader product with additional runtime, automation, and operator-control layers maintained by the RealTimeX team.

## Who’s Behind RealTimeX?

We’re the RealTimeX team, focused on making advanced AI practical to deploy and safe to operate. We care about flexibility across local and cloud runtimes, strong operational visibility, and a product surface that can work for both individual users and teams.

## Why Choose RealTimeX?

- **One control plane:** Chat, RAG, agents, local runtimes, channels, and automations live in one place.
- **Cloud or local by choice:** Use hosted APIs, self-hosted gateways, or on-device runtimes depending on the job.
- **Operator control:** Manage credentials, approvals, working directories, and runtime behavior instead of treating the agent as a black box.
- **Flexible architecture:** Mix LLMs, embedders, vector stores, plugins, and integrations to fit your environment.

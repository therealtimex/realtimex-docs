### Need a hand? No problem! You can get help in four different ways.

#### 1. GitHub Issues:

Feel free to open an issue on the RealTimeX [GitHub Repository](https://github.com/therealtimex/realtimex/issues). Both the RealTimeX, Inc Team and the RealTimeX Community are there to offer support.

#### 2. Chat with the RealTimeX, Inc Team:

Chat with us at https://realtimex.ai

#### 3. Email the RealTimeX team:

Write to us at [team@realtimex.ai](mailto:team@realtimex.ai).

#### 4. Discord Community:

Join RealTimeX, Inc Discord [Community Server](https://discord.com/invite/wWGqAjFcCz) and post your support queries on the server to get help from the community.

## RealTimeX Mobile: compatibility and known issues

RealTimeX Mobile 0.7.0 works with RealTimeX 1.2.235 or later on your desktop.

- If Chat says a desktop "needs an update", update the RealTimeX app on that desktop. Its Home items and notifications keep working meanwhile.
- To start chats from your phone, turn on Settings › Start chats from this phone for that desktop (the app also asks the first time). Replying to existing chats doesn't need it.
- Photos and files: up to 5 per message, 10 MB each; photos, PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint and text files.

### Known limitations

- Questions on Home take text answers. To answer with a photo or file, open Chat › Waiting on you.
- An unsent reply in an existing chat that has only text is kept only while the app is open. An unsent message with photos or files is kept on this phone across restarts.
- Android TalkBack: after some errors (a failed start, a stopped upload in New chat, Edit message in a chat), TalkBack says what happened, but focus can land on a nearby control. Swipe to reach Try again or the message box.
- With no workspace selected, Waiting on you counts the chats loaded so far, so it can differ from a workspace's "N waiting".
- Workspace avatars hold still on some older Android phones, and when Reduce Motion or animations are off. Each chat's state reads the same.
- If a desktop ends this phone's pairing (for example, it is revoked on the desktop), that desktop is removed from the phone. Pair it again from the desktop.
- Android TalkBack: on older phones, a swipe that scrolls the Chat list can take about 2 seconds before TalkBack speaks; other swipes are quicker. If you turn TalkBack on while the app is open, the app may not move TalkBack's cursor for you; swipe to the item you want, or close and reopen the app.
- Android TalkBack: after you close a photo or file in a chat, focus may move to "Back to Chats" a few seconds later. Swipe to get back to the file.
- Codex: the first time you start a Codex chat from your phone in a new folder, or after installing or updating RealTimeX, Codex may ask for a one-time approval on the desktop. If your phone shows the agent working but no reply comes, open that chat in RealTimeX on the desktop and answer Codex's prompt (for example, "Trust all and continue"). The chat then continues on its own.
- Codex: the first reply in a new Codex chat may appear twice. Codex answered only once, and nothing else is affected. A RealTimeX desktop update will fix this.
- Codex: if you resumed a Codex chat on the desktop and Codex offered an update there, a message you send from your phone can answer that offer. Codex updates itself and closes without replying. Send your message again.
